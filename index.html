The Locomotive Archive

A searchable archive of railroad engineering material: steam, diesel and electric locomotives, rolling stock, shop practice, standards, drawings and trade journals from many railroads, 1890s onward. About 15,600 documents and 165,000 pages, read into text so every word can be searched.

Explore the Locomotive Archive

What's here

  • Search the archive: type any word, part number, drawing number, railroad or class.
  • Steam Shop Desk: written guides for common steam locomotive repairs, each point linked to its source page, with the rules that apply where your engine runs.
  • Wiki: notes on railroads, builders, components and every source document.

The text was read from old scans by machine, so expect some garbled words. Handwriting is not readable yet. Scans of the original pages are being added; until then, search shows the text of each page.

Two references in the collection are still in copyright and are not reproduced here: the ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code, Section I (2010), available from ASME, and Machinery's Handbook (CD-ROM edition, 1998), available from Industrial Press.

This archive was given to be shared with anyone who can use it. It is offered for research and preservation. Historical shop practice is not current law: follow the rules that apply to your engine and have the work signed off by a qualified inspector.
